The Cantonment Chronicle

A Hearth Amongst the Mist

Nestled in the serene hills of Landour, Wanderroot cafe is a sanctuary for the traveler and a celebration of the local. We were born from a love of winding trails and the comfort of deep roots. Here, stories from distant lands mingle with the aroma of Himalayan soil, and every cup we pour is a tribute to the journey and the destination.
